Executive Summary

Since graduating in 2014 with my Ph.D. in Computer Science, I developed a deep knowledge of experimental design, human factors, statistical analysis, software development, and team management. I have applied these skills in technical leadership positions across several startups.

As the Co-Founder/CTO of GameWisp I grew the software team to ten individuals comprised of developers, designers, data scientists, and project managers. I led the overall technical design of GameWisp's products and shipped production-quality code on a daily basis, and grew our Annual Recurring Revenue to over $5M.

While at Codecov I served as Co-Founder/CTO and scaled the technical team from 3 to 30+ across diverse roles. I also led the overall technical direction that turned Codecov into a world class, industry leading developer tool. My efforts grew Codecov's revenue and user base many times over, and as of 2022 Codecov has been used by over a million software developers worldwide. I also conducted peer-reviewed and published research on code coverage and its usage in real engineering teams. Codecov was acquired by Sentry in 2022, where I served as Director of Engineering until 2025.

I am currently Co-Founder/CTO of Line, where I build an AI sales agent that works inbound event inquiries for hospitality venues. The agent is live at venues across the US and Canada, where it resolves more than 80% of the conversations it handles with no human involvement. It is grounded by systems I designed to verify every factual claim it makes before a guest sees it. I also built the AI-first engineering pipeline behind the product, which let a two-engineer team land over 1,600 high quality, fully reviewed pull requests in fourteen months.

I enjoy applying my skills as an executive leader, software developer, and scientist to technology companies of all sizes. I'm passionate about managing product and engineering teams, solving complex technical problems, and providing executive-level oversight to software businesses.
