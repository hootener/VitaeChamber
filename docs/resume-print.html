Executive Summary

I'm a technical co-founder with a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Vanderbilt University and over a decade of experience building and scaling software companies. Codecov was acquired by Sentry in 2022, where I continued as Director of Engineering — leading Codecov's transition from a single-purpose coverage tool to a broader code quality platform.

In 2025, I began work on Line, an AI powered deal and workflow tool for the group hospitality segment. As CTO and Co-Founder, I build the product, guide technical decision making, and assist with fundraising and overall business strategy.
